The two takes on emotion agree about 13 of 150 reviews (9%). The LLM reads tone and nuance; the NRC word list simply counts words, so it over-favors “anticipation” (common purchase words like get/will/worth are tagged with it) and struggles with sarcasm or body-vs-title tension.
